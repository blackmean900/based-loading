04Staying alive

You don't have a health bar - you have a body

  • Limbs break, arteries bleed, pain makes you shake - most deaths are a bleed-out, not a headshot
  • Bandage closes a wound, tourniquet stops arterial bleeding, bloodbag replaces lost blood
  • Painkillers hide the pain, not the wound - you can still bleed out feeling fine
  • Knocked out isn't dead - a teammate can revive you

Special rounds

  • The Brute - armoured, two-handed hammer, rips limbs off
  • Jeff - x-ray vision, blinks behind you, deadly backstab
  • SCP-173 - it only moves when nobody is looking
  • Zombie Outbreak - fight to the extraction point
  • Evolution, Mimic, Vermin, Samurai, Homelander and more - !guide lists them all
